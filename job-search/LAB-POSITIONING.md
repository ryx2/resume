## Role choices

**Strong engineering bridges:** evaluation platforms, research tools, experiment infrastructure and agent systems. These can place you close to research while using skills already visible in your background. Confirm the daily team and actual research interaction.

**Targeted research stretches:** capability evaluations, environments, agent post-training and model-behavior research engineering. Pursue these where the posting accepts relevant ML/agent experience and you can explain original hypotheses, methods and results in depth.

**Larger specialization gaps:** frontier pretraining, distributed training performance, GPU kernels and scientist roles requiring a publication record. Keep interesting opportunities visible, but evaluate their actual requirements individually. There is no need to invent a blanket PhD rule or rule yourself out by title alone.

I'm looking to bring that combination into model evaluation or research engineering, where I can own experiments and reliable systems alongside a strong technical team. I'd like to understand which group would make the best use of that background and what evidence you'd want to see.

Adapt the last sentence to the actual opening. For research tooling, emphasize researcher workflows and product craft. For model evaluation, explain how you chose a metric, found a failure and distinguished a real improvement from noise.

## Preparation that would strengthen the application

Choose one real project you can discuss without disclosing protected work. Prepare a short technical account of the problem, baseline, intervention, evaluation dataset, held-out checks, uncertainty, failure cases and your own code/design decisions. Add latency or cost tradeoffs if they affected the result. Label any new experiment as new work; do not invent ablations or metrics for earlier projects.

The most useful team questions are: Who reviews my experiments? How does this work change a model or training decision? How much time do we build together? What would I own in the first three months? These questions test whether the role delivers the learning and relationships you want before founding again.
