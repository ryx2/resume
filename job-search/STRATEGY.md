## Compare three useful paths

**Frontier model research / research engineering:** strongest when you repeatedly build and investigate alongside researchers, own experiments or reliable evaluation infrastructure, and see how model capability improves. Evaluation platforms and researcher tools can be credible engineering entry routes. Ask whether you participate in research decisions, how your work feeds training, and who reviews it. Research tooling, model research and customer deployment at the same lab can provide very different daily experiences. This path may deepen technical judgment and relationships while providing less direct exposure to discovering a new market.

**Product / applied AI engineering:** strongest when engineers own user feedback, experiments, reliability, and shipped behavior. This can develop product taste and long-term engineering collaboration. Verify that the team actually talks to users and owns outcomes.

**Forward deployed / agent engineering:** strongest when the engineer follows a customer from discovery through production and learns which problems repeat. Verify the balance of durable engineering, integration work, sales support, travel, and time with the core team. Customer access through an established company is different from finding the first customers of a new startup.

## Questions for a first conversation

1. Who would I work with most days, and who would review my code and technical decisions?
2. Could you walk me through one recent project from the first user conversation to a few months after launch? What did the team get wrong initially?
3. What would I personally own in the first three months, and how would we know it worked?
4. How much of a normal month is building, talking to customers, internal meetings, travel, and production support?
5. What customer problems recur, and how much can this team change the product rather than implement one-off work?
6. What kind of engineer thrives here, and what is the most frustrating part of the role?

After promising conversations, meet the actual prospective manager and close peers. Record concrete examples and remaining unknowns before ranking offers.
